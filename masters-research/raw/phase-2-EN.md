## ⚠️ DECISIVE FUNDING REALITY — read first (honesty guardrail)

The two schemes named as first priority in the brief have a **hard blocker** for this candidate:

- **Chevening (UK)** requires **at least 2 years / 2,800 hours of work experience** *(work before, during, or after the undergrad all counts, incl. internships/part-time/voluntary)*. Chevening explicitly states that someone who finished their undergraduate degree too recently "won't have had enough time to meet the work experience requirement." A candidate **finishing** Systems Engineering in 2026 with little accumulated work experience is **likely ineligible for the 2027 cycle** (opens ~5 Aug 2026, closes ~7 Oct 2026, *secondary*). If she has 2,800 h of internship/part-time/dance-teaching/paid work accumulated, she may qualify — she must count her hours. Source (secondary): https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/who-can-apply/work-experience/
- **Fulbright Colombia (US)** master call requires a professional degree **+ at least 1 year of professional experience**; call opens **2 Mar 2026**, closes **15 May 2026** (11:59 pm Colombia), for a program starting **second semester of 2027**. *(secondary)* Source: https://fulbright.edu.co/en/ · https://www.eltiempo.com/vida/educacion/fulbright-colombia-amplia-fecha-de-cierre-de-convocatoria-para-posgrados-en-estados-unidos-486950
  - **Timing problem:** Fulbright's deadline (May 2026) is **before** her "execution date" of July 2026. For a Sept-2027 start the relevant Fulbright cycle would essentially already be closing as she begins. Verify the exact class-year on the official call.

**What this means:** the realistic **funded** routes for a fresh graduate with **no work-experience gate** are:
1. **Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (EMJM)** — €1,400/month + tuition + travel, **no work experience required**, apply directly to the consortium (deadlines typically **Oct 2026–Feb 2027** for Sept-2027 start). *Top priority for her profile.*

Note the flagship **DAAD EPOS** is aimed at applicants with **~2 years professional experience** in development-related fields — same work-experience caveat; other DAAD lines (e.g. WISE, or program-specific) do not all require it — verify per call. DAAD EPOS 2027/28 deadlines fall **Jun–Oct 2026**, set per university. Source (secondary): https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?detail=50076777
3. **Netherlands** — Radboud Scholarship / NL Scholarship (partial), Holland Scholarship.
4. **MEXT (Japan)** and **GKS (Korea)** — government scholarships that **do NOT require prior work experience** and accept fresh graduates; strong fits (details below).
