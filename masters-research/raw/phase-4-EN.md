## B.2 — CHEVENING: PRECISE VERIFICATION

All from chevening.org pages surfaced via search [secondary source — official pages, validity 2026]:

- **Requirement: 2,800 hours** of work experience (≈2 years at 35 h/week), completed **before submitting the application**. Source: https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/who-can-apply/work-experience/
- **What counts:** full-time, part-time, voluntary work, **paid or unpaid internships**, freelancing, self-employment; up to 15 employment periods can be combined. Source: https://www.chevening.org/news/everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-chevening-work-experience-requirement/
- **Timing relative to degree — the decisive nuance:** official FAQ guidance indicates experience gained before/during/after undergraduate studies counts, **except employment undertaken as a mandatory credited part of the course**. One page states hours "acquired after completion of your undergraduate degree" — the two official texts conflict; **this exact point must be confirmed on the 2027-cycle FAQ**: https://www.chevening.org/faqs/can-i-apply-for-a-chevening-award-in-the-final-year-of-my-undergraduate-degree/ and https://www.chevening.org/faqs/what-types-of-work-experience-are-accepted/
- **2027 cycle dates:** applications expected **4 Aug – 6 Oct 2026**; unconditional UK offer needed by 8 Jul 2027. Sources: https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/application-timeline/ (+ aggregators, validity 2026)
- **Degree subjects:** no restriction by undergraduate field (any master's subject) — standard Chevening policy; verify on eligibility page https://www.chevening.org/resource-hub/guidance/eligibility/

**Verdict for Paula:** NOT automatically out of reach. If her non-curricular work (e.g. part-time software engineering at Avatar during studies: 20 h/week × ~2.7 years ≈ 2,800 h) is documentable and was not degree-credited, she could apply in **Aug–Oct 2026** for 2027. Action: compute her real hours this August against the calculator on the work-experience page. If short → 2028 cycle. **SISGP (Sweden) confirmed to still require 3,000 hours** for Colombia applicants (source: https://si.se/en/apply/scholarships/swedish-institute-scholarships-for-global-professionals/ [secondary, validity 2026]) — same calculation applies; slightly higher bar.

- **3 years in (typical outcomes):** data scientist / economist at central banks, economic-consulting firms, tech (pricing, marketplaces, causal inference), multilaterals (IDB/World Bank — Latin American profile is an asset there). **Salary estimates (unsourced, confidence LOW, 2026):** NL/DE entry €40–60k; UK £35–50k; US econ consulting $80–110k. 2026 market: causal-inference + ML hybrid profiles in demand; pure-theory profiles are not.
